Privacy notice

We only use your details to answer your enquiry. We don't sell them, share them for marketing, or use tracking cookies.

Who we are

This website is run by M. Wallace Memorials, monumental sculptor, based in Kinross-shire (KY13). We are responsible for the details you give us on this website.

Questions about your data: mwallacememorials@gmail.com or 07827 665296.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry, we keep:

  • your name, email address and phone number;
  • the work you'd like done, the cemetery or churchyard, and which page you sent it from;
  • anything you write in the message box, and any photos of the stone you add.

Browsing the site doesn't collect anything about you. Nothing is sent until you press Send my enquiry. Photos you send are private: only our staff can see them.

Why we use it

To look at the work, give you a price and arrange it with you. The legal basis is that you've asked us to take steps towards a contract with you. We won't add you to a mailing list.

Who handles it for us

We use a small number of service providers to run the site. They process data only on our instructions:

  • Supabase stores your enquiry, your photos and the job photos shown on the site (our database).
  • Resend sends us an email when a new enquiry arrives, containing the details you entered and links to your photos.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and, like any web host, briefly logs visitors' IP addresses for security.
  • Google Fonts serves the fonts, so it sees your IP address when the page loads.
  • If you choose to contact us on WhatsApp, that conversation is covered by WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

Some of these providers may store data outside the UK. Where they do, they use the safeguards UK data protection law requires, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries and their photos for 12 months so we can deal with follow-up questions, then delete them. If you go ahead with the work, we keep the job details for as long as the law requires for our business records (usually 6 years).

Cookies and storage

This site doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. The staff login page stores a sign-in token in the browser, and that's only used by our staff.

Job photos and reviews

We only publish photos of a memorial and a family's review with that family's agreement, and we show reviewers by first name and initial or area. Ask us and we'll take them down.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your details, to correct them, or to delete them, and you can object to how we use them. Email or phone us using the details above. If you're not happy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
